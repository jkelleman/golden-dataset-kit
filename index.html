Golden Dataset Kit

The answer key for an AI support agent.

A binary rubric, 24 labeled conversations, a two-rater agreement check and a release gate for an LLM judge. Browse the labels, read the rubric and see whether the sample judge is ready to ship.

  • 24 labeled conversations

Rater agreement

Two people label the same turns before anyone trusts the rubric. Kappa corrects for agreement by chance. Below 0.6, the criterion needs clearer boundaries before a judge can learn it.

Judge readiness

The sample judge's verdicts scored against the golden labels. Fail recall is what matters for critical criteria: of the turns people failed, how many did the judge catch? Move the gates to see what ships.
